# Evals: humanize-prose

Grade the rewritten text against each check. Pass or fail, no scores.
Any failure: return the text with the failed checks listed and the offending words quoted.

## Tells
- [ ] No em dash or en dash appears, except inside a numeric or date range.
- [ ] No "it's not X, it's Y" or "not just X, but Y" construction appears.
- [ ] No word or stock phrase from `references/tells.md` appears.
- [ ] No triad of adjectives, benefits or verbs appears (a list of three distinct named things is allowed).
- [ ] The last sentence does not restate an earlier one.

## Rhythm
- [ ] No three consecutive sentences are within four words of each other in length.
- [ ] No two consecutive sentences start with the same word.

## Voice
- [ ] The first sentence states the point or the ask, not background.
- [ ] None of moreover, furthermore, additionally, consequently, thus or hence appears.
- [ ] No "it depends", "on the other hand" or "that said" appears unless the notes list it under "Kept on purpose".

## Fidelity (needs the original; the producer checks these, not the clean grader)
- [ ] Every fact, number and name in the rewrite appears in the original.
- [ ] No fact, number or name from the original is missing, other than a deleted summary closer or cut hedge.
